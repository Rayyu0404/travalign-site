Privacy Policy

Applies to the mobile app Travalign (行理)

Last updated: 30 September 2026

Scope

This policy applies to the mobile app Travalign (行理). Travalign has no server and no account system, and its developer does not collect or keep any user's personal data.

The trips, places, expenses, member names, tickets and attachments, place book and settings you create in Travalign live only on your own phone.

What Travalign collects

Nothing. Itineraries, expenses, members, attachments, the place book and settings stay on this phone and are not sent to the developer. There are no analytics, ads or tracking tools in the app.

Data categories: trip and itinerary content, expenses and member names, tickets and files you add, your list of places, and your preferences. There is one purpose: to show and calculate things for you on this phone.

Where the app connects

Only the four things below make Travalign connect on its own. Every other web address you see is a link that opens in your browser or another app only when you tap it (for example Google Maps directions, government and shop websites, and data sources); Travalign never connects to those by itself.

・Viewing the online map: connects to OpenFreeMap (tiles.openfreemap.org) to load map tiles. That server can see your phone's network address and the map area you are viewing. Downloaded offline maps need no connection.
・Downloading offline maps: connects to GitHub (the github.com and githubusercontent.com domains). The map files sit in a public GitHub project of the developer. The request carries none of your data; GitHub can see your phone's network address.
・Refreshing exchange rates: connects to open.er-api.com and sends only currency codes, never itineraries or expenses.
・Sharing a Google Maps short link into Travalign: the phone opens that link to see where it points.

None of these four carries your itinerary, expenses or name.

Automatic phone backup

Android has a system backup feature. When it is on, the phone copies Travalign's data (tickets and attachments included) to your own Google account. That is between your phone and Google; the developer cannot get it. If you do not want it, turn backup off in the phone's settings.

What each permission is for

・Location: read once, only when you tap "My location" and only while Travalign is open. It is not tracked, stored or sent anywhere.
・Camera: only for scanning QR codes; the image is processed on the phone and not uploaded.
・Calendar: only adds, changes and removes the events Travalign itself put there; it does not read the rest of your calendar.
・Notifications: reminders such as departure and meeting times, scheduled on the phone itself.
・Exact alarms: so a reminder goes off at the exact minute you set.
・Reschedule after boot: after the phone restarts, puts reminders that have not gone off back in place.

What you share yourself

Files made with "Pack this trip", QR, or "Save as Image, PDF, or Excel" are created only when you tap, and you choose who gets them (for example LINE or email). Once a file has been sent, what the other person does with it is outside Travalign's reach.

When you open Google Maps for directions, Travalign puts the destination (sometimes your current position) into the link it hands to Google Maps. That also happens only when you tap, and Google's own privacy policy applies from there.

How long data is kept and how to delete it

Data stays until you delete it. You can delete trips one by one, clear attachments trip by trip, delete offline maps, or tap "Delete all data" at the bottom of Me → Storage to clear everything Travalign holds on this phone, settings included.

Uninstalling Travalign also removes this data. A copy already backed up to your Google account has to be deleted separately in that account.

Your rights (notice under Taiwan's Personal Data Protection Act)

Travalign does not collect personal data and the developer holds none of yours. Data is used on this phone only, by you only, by showing and calculating on the phone, for as long as you keep it.

Access, viewing, copying, supplementing or correcting, stopping use, and deletion are all things you can do yourself inside Travalign, without asking anyone.

Children

Travalign is not designed for children and does not collect personal data from anyone, children included.
